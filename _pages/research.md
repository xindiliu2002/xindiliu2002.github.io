My research interests lie at the intersection of **empirical industrial organization**, **digital economics**, and **applied microeconomics**. I am particularly interested in how digital technologies, data regulation, and platform markets reshape firm behavior, market structure, and economic outcomes.

## Current Research

### Data Privacy Regulation and Market Concentration

My master's thesis examines whether data privacy regulation increases market concentration, using the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as an empirical setting. This project investigates how regulatory compliance costs, data access restrictions, and changes in digital advertising and platform governance may affect the competitive positions of firms with different sizes and data-processing capabilities.

### Managerial Disclosure and Investment Efficiency

I am also working on a project on managerial excessive tone and corporate investment efficiency in China. This study examines whether unusually positive managerial tone in annual reports serves as credible information transmission rather than impression management. Using textual information from MD&A disclosures of Chinese A-share listed firms, the project studies how managerial disclosure is associated with under-investment, over-investment, risk-taking, and internal control quality.

## Broader Research Agenda

More broadly, I am interested in digital markets, platform competition, firm strategy, and the economic consequences of data governance. I aim to use micro-level data and applied econometric methods to study how firms and markets respond to digital transformation and regulatory change.
