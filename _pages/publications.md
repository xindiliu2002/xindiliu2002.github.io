## Research in Progress

**Artificial Intelligence and Employment Stability**
This project examines how firm-level artificial intelligence adoption affects employment stability in China, with a focus on employment volatility, labor-demand adjustment, job restructuring, and heterogeneity across industries, firm size, skill structure, and digital intensity.

**Data Privacy Regulation and Market Concentration**
This project examines whether data privacy regulation increases market concentration, using the GDPR as an empirical setting.
