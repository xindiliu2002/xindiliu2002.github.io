--- title: "Teaching & Writing" permalink: /teaching-writing/ author_profile: true --- ## Textbook Development ### *Digital Economics*, 2nd ed. **Contributing Writer** China Machine Press, publication planned for 2026 I drafted and substantially revised more than half of the manuscript, with formal acknowledgment of my contribution planned in the book's preface. My work involved systematically reviewing foundational literature in information economics, the knowledge economy, and digital economics, and reorganizing the material into a coherent textbook framework. The chapters I worked on cover digital goods, price discrimination, e-commerce, the sharing economy, network effects, platform pricing, and platform monopoly. I adapted canonical economic models into simplified derivations and accessible explanations for classroom use. ### *Introduction to the Digital Economy* **Contributing Writer** Peking University Press, publication planned for 2026 I authored a four-chapter thematic module on the evolution of digital products and business models from the second half of the twentieth century to the present. The module discusses representative business models in e-commerce, the sharing economy, and platform markets, and explains their underlying economic mechanisms for undergraduate readers. ## Selected Public Writing I also write occasional essays and reading notes on topics related to the philosophy of science, social science methodology, and the intellectual foundations of economic inquiry.
